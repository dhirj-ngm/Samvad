# Samvad three-minute demo

Candidate: `release/p6-demo`. Use the exact private account mapping; A reads
Malayalam, B Hindi, C English and D is the separately authorized operator. Keep
two phones ready on the same reachable network. All prepared fictional content
is labeled; cached AI results are identified as cache, never described as a new
live generation. Automatic publication stays OFF.

| Time | Show | Say / acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Android and iPhone open Home, different ordinary accounts | “Samvad is a public conversation prototype for people across languages. Your public handle is separate from your private email.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Attributed archive news, source link, interest filter | Explain sourced news versus labeled fictional posts. Open the source link and return; no claim of live news ingestion. |
| 0:45–1:25 | T1 Malayalam bus discussion on the English/Hindi reader's phone; Translate, Show original, nested reply | “The original stays intact. This prepared translation is a real saved cache result.” Check Indic script, authors and stable thread identity on both phones. |
| 1:25–1:50 | T2 English proposal, English/native summary | Describe a summary of the original post, not the whole discussion. If cache unavailable, show the explicit error and original; do not invent a result. |
| 1:50–2:15 | Public profile, follow/like/repost, refresh, Following | Show persisted state and original attribution. Keep email/account settings private. |
| 2:15–2:45 | Ordinary account saves one benign reply; private pending state. D reviews that exact source; other phone refreshes | “While our language/policy gate is open, new content needs trusted human publication review.” Use only a known benign submission; don't approve queue items indiscriminately. |
| 2:45–3:00 | Original text after an AI error, or retry guidance | “Provider failures preserve originals and pending writes. This demo uses a laptop-attended local backend; production rollout and a real consenting-user pilot are separate.” |

If a network or quota failure occurs, say so and show existing originals. Do not
enable paid fallback, change providers, bypass authorization, or refill persistent
budgets to rescue the presentation. If time is short, omit a new live submission
and show the already reviewed thread, labeling it as prepared.

Capture one Home/source view, a translation with Show original, an Indic reply,
a public profile and the opposite-device thread view. Keep reviewer/private
screens out of public screenshots. Record the rehearsal outcome separately for
Android and iPhone in [P6 verification](p6-verification.md).
